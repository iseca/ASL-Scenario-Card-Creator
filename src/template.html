Click any text on the card to edit it, including the section headings. Drop images onto the logo, badge, photo and mapboard boxes. Hover a section for its controls (move it, start it in the right column, remove it); hover the mission tables to add or remove rows and columns. Press Enter in a bulleted or numbered section to start the next item. In any text, Outline • Bullet adds or removes a bullet; Tab / Shift+Tab (or → / ←) move it a level in or out. Counter palette adds counters from your VASL module to an OB row (hover the row, click Counters…); Board layout builds the mapboard diagram from your VASL boards.

Click any text on the card to edit it. Drop images onto the photo, counter boxes, board box, logo and side art, or click a box to choose a file. Hover a counter, counter row or OB panel for its controls; drag counters to reorder them. Click a turn box to mark reinforcements, shading or a half-turn game end. Counter palette adds counters straight from your VASL module: hover a counter row and click Counters…, then click or drag counters into it. Board layout (or hover the board box) builds the board configuration from your VASL boards and overlays.

    Counter palette

    The palette uses the counters in your own copy of VASL. Choose your VASL module file (for example vasl-6.7.3.vmod) once; it is read here in your browser and kept with your saved cards, nothing is uploaded.

    When a new VASL comes out, load the new file the same way and the palette is rebuilt from it. Counters already on your cards don't change.
